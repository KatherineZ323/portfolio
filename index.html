About Me

Where data meets
product

I’m naturally drawn to problems that sit between data, technology, and user experience.

I like figuring out what the data is telling us, what users actually need, and how those insights can come together in a better product. My approach often starts with research and analysis — using tools like SQL, Python, and R to uncover patterns, evaluate opportunities, and support product decisions.

From there, I use Figma and rapid prototyping to turn insights into user flows, product concepts, and interactive experiences that teams can test and build. My work spans product analytics, UX research, data visualization, AI product development, and cross-functional collaboration with engineers and stakeholders.

I recently graduated from Cornell University with an MPS in Information Science, building on a B.S. in Informatics from the University of Washington. Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, skiing, traveling, and discovering new food. And I’m a huge animal person! I love dogs and cats.
